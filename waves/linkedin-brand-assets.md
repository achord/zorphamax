# LinkedIn Brand Asset Guide

For a LinkedIn company brand kit, these are the primary assets to create.

> **Note:** LinkedIn currently recommends a **1512 × 256 px** company Page cover image. Some older template libraries may still show **1128 × 191 px**.

| Asset | Size | Ratio | What to Create | Template / Reference |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| **Company Logo / Icon** | **400 × 400 px** | 1:1 | Symbol/logo optimized to work small | [LinkedIn official specs](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a567368) |
| **Company Cover / Banner** | **1512 × 256 px** | ~5.9:1 | Brand statement, product imagery, tagline | [LinkedIn official specs](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a567368) |
| **Personal Profile Photo** | **400 × 400 px** | 1:1 | Headshot, if branding executives/team | [Figma LinkedIn size guide](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/linkedin-size-guide/) |

## Recommended Starter Kit

For Maverix, I would start with these six master assets:

1. **Logo / icon** — 400 × 400 px
2. **Company cover** — 1512 × 256 px
3. **Employee / personal cover** — 1584 × 396 px
4. **Square social template** — 1200 × 1200 px
5. **Landscape social template** — 1200 × 628 px
6. **Carousel template** — 1080 × 1080 px

## Suggested Social Template Variants

Build the social assets as a reusable Figma component system with variants for:

- Product Feature
- Announcement
- Quote / Stat
- Event
- Customer Story
- Hiring

## Cover Image Safe Area

For the **1512 × 256 px company cover**, keep important typography and logos toward the center.

LinkedIn can crop the cover differently depending on screen size, particularly near the edges and lower-right area.

Reference: [LinkedIn cover image guidance](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a7433061)
